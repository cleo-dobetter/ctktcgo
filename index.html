      HOW TO PLAY

      1. Goal: Reduce enemy HP to 0.

      2. Energy: You have 2 Actions per turn.

      - Attacks hit the card in front of them.
      - If no card is in front, they hit the Player directly.
      - Defense cards deal "Thorn Damage" if Attack < Defense.
